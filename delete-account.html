Delete your Gravitoa account

Last updated: November 21, 2026

App: Gravitoa (Android)

Developer: Deniz Doğan

You can delete your Gravitoa account and the data stored with it at any time, either inside the app or by email. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what may be kept. For the full policy, see our Privacy Policy.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Gravitoa and go to the Profile screen.
  2. Choose Delete account.

Deletion starts immediately. It deletes your account and the data described below, including archived chat messages, and unregisters your push notification token.

Option 2: Delete by email

  1. From the email address registered to your Gravitoa account, write to dnzdggn+gravitoa@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Gravitoa account".

We complete deletion requested by email within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your account and everything we store about you in Cloud Firestore, including:

  • Your profile and onboarding answers (name, age, city, occupation, situation, alcohol yes/no, hangout spots, preferences)
  • Your mood and check-in records, journal entries and panic-flow records
  • Your mentor chat messages, including archived chat messages, and the daily, weekly and monthly summaries made from them
  • Your weekly plans and insight reports
  • Your likes and dislikes on daily content, your points and level, and your daily AI-usage counter
  • Your subscription record (tier, status, expiry, billing period and Google Play purchase token) and your ad reward credits

Your push notification token is also unregistered.

What may be retained

  • Data we are required to keep by law.
  • Short-lived technical logs that contain no message content.
  • Anonymous aggregate counts on shared content (for example like counts).
  • Purchase records held by Google Play.
  • Data held by Google and OpenAI under their own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and OpenAI Privacy Policy. Deleting your Gravitoa account does not automatically delete your Google account or data held by those companies.
  • Backups, which may persist briefly before they are overwritten.

How long it takes

  • In the app: deletion starts immediately.
  • By email: completed within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. If you have a subscription, manage or cancel it in Google Play.
