For privacy questions or requests, email us at dnzdggn+gravitoa@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Gravitoa is an AI chat companion app for Android, for adults aged 18 and over. Depending on how you use it, we collect the following information. The information you give us, your subscription information and your notification token are stored in Cloud Firestore and tied to your account.

Information you give us

  • Account data: your email address and your sign-in method (email and password, or Google Sign-In), handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile and onboarding answers: your name, age, city (as you type it), occupation, situation, whether you drink alcohol (yes or no), hangout spots and preferences.
  • Mood and check-in records: the mood and check-in information you record in the app. We treat this as sensitive wellbeing information (see section 7).
  • Journal entries: the entries you write in the app's journal.
  • Panic-flow records: records created when you use the app's panic flow.
  • Mentor chat content: the messages you send in the app's mentor chat, and the daily, weekly and monthly summaries made from them. Messages and summaries are sent from our backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) to OpenAI's API to generate replies (see section 4), and are stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Plans and reports: your weekly plans, and insight reports (paid tiers).
  • Activity in the app: your likes and dislikes on daily content, your points and level, and a daily AI-usage counter.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification tokens: a token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) so we can send notifications to your device.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, ad interaction data, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and device information in order to show ads.

Subscription information

  • Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We store your subscription tier, status, expiry, billing period and Google Play purchase token, as well as any ad reward credits you have earned. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the chat service: to receive your messages and generate AI replies.
  • Run your account and the app's features: to sign you in, and to keep your profile, check-ins, journal entries, panic-flow records, weekly plans, insight reports, likes and dislikes, and points and level, and to keep your data available across sessions.
  • Personalization: to tailor the experience using the profile information and chat summaries you have provided.
  • Show ads: to display advertising in the app, subject to your consent choices (see section 6).
  • Manage subscriptions: to know your subscription tier and whether it is active, and unlock the related features.
  • Safety and security: to protect the app and its users, prevent abuse and fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Notifications and support: to send push notifications and to respond to your privacy requests.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example under the GDPR), we rely on performing our service to you (contract), your consent (for example for personalized ads and, where applicable, notifications), our legitimate interests (for example security and running the app), and legal obligations. In Türkiye, processing is carried out under the conditions set out in the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

4. AI processing and OpenAI

Gravitoa's replies are generated by artificial intelligence. When you chat, your chat content and the summaries made from it are sent from our servers to OpenAI, acting as a data processor on our behalf, so that it can generate responses. The app never calls OpenAI directly from your device: messages go to our backend on Firebase Cloud Functions, which contacts OpenAI.

We do not currently use users' content to train AI models. If that ever changes, we will ask for your separate opt-in consent first.

Please do not share information in chats that you do not want processed in this way. This includes information about other people, and any details you would not be comfortable having handled by an AI service provider.

How OpenAI handles data that it receives through its API is described in its own published policies. We do not make any claims about OpenAI's training or retention practices beyond pointing you to those documents:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API data usage policy ("Data controls in the OpenAI platform")

AI-generated replies can be inaccurate, incomplete or inappropriate. Please use your own judgment.

6. Advertising and consent choices

Gravitoa shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID, ad interaction data, approximate location derived from your IP address, and device information to serve ads, and ads may be personalized.

  • Consent prompt: if you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or another region where it is required, the app shows a consent prompt powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice later in the app where the option is provided.
  • Personalized vs. non-personalized ads: if you decline personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your advertising profile.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, go to your device's Settings and look for the Privacy (or Google → Ads) section, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization. Exact menu names vary by device and Android version.
  • Google's controls: you can also manage ad personalization in your Google account's ad settings.

More information: How Google uses data for advertising.

7. Sensitive information and wellbeing disclaimer

Gravitoa is a chat companion that can touch personal and mental-health-related topics, and you may record mood and check-in entries, journal entries and panic-flow records. This kind of information can be sensitive. We handle it only to provide the app's features as described in this policy, and you choose what to share.

Gravitoa is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It is not a substitute for a doctor, psychologist, therapist or other qualified professional. Do not ignore professional advice or delay seeking it because of something the app said.

If you are in crisis or think you may harm yourself or others, please contact your local emergency services right away. In the United States you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Outside the USA, call your local emergency number (for example 112 in the EU and Türkiye) or find a local helpline at findahelpline.com.

8. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account in the app, deletion starts immediately. If you ask us to delete it by email, we complete the deletion within 30 days. Some limited data may be retained after deletion, for example data we must keep by law, and backups may persist for a short time before they are overwritten. See section 10 and our account deletion page for details.

Data held by third parties such as Google and OpenAI is kept under their own policies (see section 5).

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correction: ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data (you can also edit your profile in the app).
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data (see section 10).
  • Objection / restriction: object to or ask us to limit certain processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent you have given, such as for personalized ads or notifications. This does not affect processing done before you withdrew.
  • Portability: ask for your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format where this right applies.

EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the rights listed above. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

California (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you can ask to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and you will not be treated unfairly for exercising these rights. Depending on how the law is applied, showing personalized ads through an advertising network may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising; you can opt out by declining personalized ads or resetting your advertising ID as described in section 6, or by emailing us.

Türkiye (KVKK)

Under Article 11 of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you can ask whether your data is processed, request information about it, learn its purpose and whether it is used appropriately, learn who it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, ask for correction or deletion, ask that these actions be notified to third parties the data was transferred to, object to results against you produced solely by automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr).

How to exercise your rights

Email us at dnzdggn+gravitoa@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account and tell us what you would like to do. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We aim to respond within a reasonable time and within any period required by applicable law.

10. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time. A dedicated page with these steps is available at Delete your Gravitoa account.

  1. In the app: open the Profile screen and choose Delete account. Deletion starts immediately.
  2. By email: send an email to dnzdggn+gravitoa@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Gravitoa account", from the email address registered to your Gravitoa account. We complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your account and everything we store about you in Cloud Firestore, including:

  • Your profile and onboarding answers
  • Your mood and check-in records, journal entries and panic-flow records
  • Your mentor chat messages, including archived chat messages, and the daily, weekly and monthly summaries made from them
  • Your weekly plans and insight reports
  • Your likes and dislikes on daily content, your points and level, and your daily AI-usage counter
  • Your subscription record (tier, status, expiry, billing period and Google Play purchase token) and your ad reward credits

Your push notification token is also unregistered.

What may be retained

  • Data we are required to keep by law.
  • Short-lived technical logs that contain no message content.
  • Anonymous aggregate counts on shared content (for example like counts).
  • Purchase records held by Google Play.
  • Data held by Google and OpenAI under their own policies (see section 5). Deleting your Gravitoa account does not automatically delete your Google account or data held by those companies.
  • Backups, which may persist briefly before they are overwritten.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your sign-in credentials safe.

If you are in Türkiye, transfers abroad are made in line with KVKK requirements.
